Composite Materials

Explore commonly used materials in composite engineering.

◼

Carbon Fiber

Carbon fiber composites are known for their high strength-to-weight ratio and stiffness.

Applications: Aerospace, automotive and sports equipment.

◻

Glass Fiber

Glass fiber composites provide good mechanical properties at relatively low cost.

Applications: Boats, construction and automotive components.

◆

Aramid Fiber

Aramid fibers offer high toughness and good resistance to impact.

Applications: Protective equipment and aerospace components.

🧪

Epoxy Resin

Epoxy resins are commonly used as matrix materials in high-performance composites.

Applications: Aerospace, electronics and structural components.

⚗️

Polyester Resin

Polyester resin is widely used because of its affordability and ease of processing.

Applications: Marine, automotive and construction.

🔗

Hybrid Composites

Hybrid composites combine two or more types of reinforcement to achieve desired properties.

Applications: Advanced structural applications.
