About the Platform

Understanding composite materials through technology.

Composite Materials Engineering Platform

The Composite Materials Engineering Platform is a college project designed to provide a simple digital environment for exploring composite materials and their engineering applications.

The platform currently focuses on presenting materials, manufacturing processes and basic engineering tools through an easy-to-use web interface.

Future versions of the platform can include engineering calculations, databases and machine learning models for property prediction and material analysis.
