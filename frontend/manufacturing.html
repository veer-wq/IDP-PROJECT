Manufacturing Processes

Explore common manufacturing techniques used for composite components.

🏭

Hand Lay-Up

A simple manufacturing process where reinforcement materials are manually placed and impregnated with resin.

⚙️

Compression Molding

Composite material is placed inside a mold and compressed under controlled pressure and temperature.

🔄

Filament Winding

Continuous fibers are wound around a rotating mandrel to manufacture cylindrical structures.

💨

Vacuum Infusion

Vacuum pressure is used to draw resin through dry reinforcement materials.

🏗️

Resin Transfer Molding

Resin is injected into a mold containing reinforcement material.

🔬

Autoclave Processing

Composite laminates are cured using controlled temperature and pressure inside an autoclave.
